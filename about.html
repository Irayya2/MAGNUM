What is MAGNUM?

KLS Gogte College of Commerce presents MAGNUM 2K26 — a grand national-level IT & Cultural Fest. An arena where teams from across colleges gather to showcase technical innovation, coding prowess, creative mastery, and competitive spirit.

About KLS GCC

KLS Gogte College of Commerce (GCC), Belagavi, under Karnataka Law Society, is an institution accredited with "A" grade by NAAC. The Department of BCA empowers students with cutting-edge technical education, practical exposure, and industry readiness.
